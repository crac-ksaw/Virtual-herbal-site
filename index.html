🌟 Featured Plant of the Week

Mint (Mentha)

Mint is a refreshing herb known for its cooling flavor and aroma. It’s used in teas, desserts, and as a natural remedy for digestion and stress relief.

  • 🌱 Easy to grow indoors and outdoors
  • 🍃 Great for teas and drinks
  • 🪴 Repels some pests naturally

Quick Plant Tips

💧 Water most houseplants when the top inch of soil is dry.
🌞 Place sun-loving plants near south-facing windows.
🌱 Prune dead leaves to encourage new growth.
🪴 Repot plants every 1-2 years for healthy roots.
🐛 Check for pests regularly and act early.
🌿 Rotate your plants for even sunlight exposure.
